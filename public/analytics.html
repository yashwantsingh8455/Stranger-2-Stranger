How unique counting works: login is not required. The landing page gives each browser profile a persistent visitor ID. The server stores only its SHA-256 hash. Repeated page loads update visit activity but do not increase the all-time unique visitor count. A new calendar year needs no manual setup: when visits arrive after January 1, that year appears automatically.
